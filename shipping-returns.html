Shipping & Returns

Details below are indicative and will be confirmed at launch.

Shipping

We'll launch with delivery across Singapore. Standard delivery is expected to take 2–4 business days, with free shipping over a minimum spend. More regions will follow after launch.

Returns

Unworn items with original tags can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. For hygiene reasons, socks and intimate items may be non-returnable unless faulty.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Reach out and we'll help you exchange, subject to stock availability.

Faulty items

If something arrives faulty, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll make it right.
