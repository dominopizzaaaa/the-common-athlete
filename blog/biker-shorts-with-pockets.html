Why Biker Shorts With Pockets Belong in Your Rotation

29 May 2026 · 3 min read

If one piece bridges the gap between training and real life, it's a good pair of high-waisted biker shorts. Add pockets, and they quietly become the most-worn thing in your drawer.

Squat-proof where it counts

The first test of any training short is whether it stays opaque when you move. A thicker, supportive fabric with the right stretch means no see-through moments at the bottom of a squat — so you can train without thinking about it.

Pockets that actually hold your phone

Side pockets turn a workout short into an everyday short. Slide in your phone for a treadmill walk, keys for a quick errand, or a card for the coffee run after. It's a small feature that changes how often you reach for them.

High-waisted for comfort and hold

A high waist sits comfortably, stays put through movement, and pairs cleanly with a cropped top or an oversized tee. Soft compression adds a smoothing, supported feel without digging in.

One short, many uses

  • Strength and conditioning sessions
  • Walks, Pilates, and light runs
  • Errands and casual days
  • Layered under a dress or oversized tee
