Why Shorts With Pockets Belong in Your Rotation

29 May 2026 · 3 min read

If one piece bridges the gap between training and real life, it's a good pair of high-waisted shorts with pockets. We make two — the fitted V-Crossover and the 2-in-1 Flowy Running Short — and between them they cover almost everything.

Squat-proof where it counts

The first test of any training short is whether it stays opaque when you move. The High-Waist V-Crossover Shorts use a thicker, supportive fabric with the right stretch — no see-through moments at the bottom of a squat, so you can train without thinking about it.

Pockets that actually hold your phone

Side pockets turn a workout short into an everyday short. The V-Crossover has sleek side pockets for your phone and keys; the 2-in-1 Running Short adds a side phone pocket plus a secure zip back pocket for easy miles. It's a small feature that changes how often you reach for them.

Fitted or flowy — take your pick

Want compression and a smoothing, supported feel? The V-Crossover sits high with a flattering crossover waistband. Prefer airflow and coverage on a run? The 2-in-1 layers a flowy outer over a fitted liner — no chafe, nothing riding up. Both stay put through movement.

One short, many uses

  • Strength and conditioning sessions (V-Crossover)
  • Runs, walks, and warm-weather training (2-in-1)
  • Errands and casual days
  • Layered under a dress or oversized layer
