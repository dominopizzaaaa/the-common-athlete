What to Wear to the Gym in Singapore's Heat

12 June 2026 · 4 min read

Training in Singapore means training in humidity. The right activewear won't make the heat disappear — but it will keep you cooler, drier, and more comfortable from your warm-up to your last set.

Choose breathable, sweat-friendly fabrics

In a humid climate, fabric does most of the work. Look for lightweight, sweat-wicking materials with a bit of stretch — they move moisture away from your skin so you don't feel weighed down mid-session. Avoid heavy cotton that soaks and stays wet.

Pick fits that move with you

Compression that's snug but not restrictive helps you feel supported without overheating. High-waisted biker shorts and a low-to-medium support crop are ideal for strength days, Pilates, and walks alike. The goal is pieces you forget you're wearing.

Layer light

An oversized cover tee is perfect for the walk to the gym or as a pump cover — then it comes off when you start to sweat. One light layer beats one heavy one, every time.

A simple heat-friendly checklist

  • Sweat-wicking, breathable fabric over heavy cotton
  • Low-to-medium support crop with removable padding
  • High-waisted shorts with pockets for your phone and keys
  • A light layer you can take off easily
  • Neutral colours that don't show sweat as obviously

None of this requires a luxury budget. Functional, well-made basics will outperform flashy pieces in a Singapore gym every time.
